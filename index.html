The Story

Resilience isn’t built in comfort. It’s forged under iron, through pain, and with zero excuses.

Above-Knee Amputee. Lifetime Natural. Still Grinding.

In July 2004, a car accident took my right leg and nearly took my life. A stranger used his belt as a tourniquet. Days in a coma. Years learning to walk again while fighting chronic osteomyelitis.

Instead of stopping, I turned pain into power. Over 27 years of natural bodybuilding. Regular 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place finishes in Men’s Physique. Adaptive athlete with osseointegration. Daily antibiotics. Zero excuses.

“Don’t talk about it. Be about it.”
“You didn’t break. You kept going. That’s the real superpower.”

This isn’t just about muscle. It’s about the mental battle. Showing up when it’s easier to stay down. Building a physique and a life that lasts—no matter what life throws at you.
